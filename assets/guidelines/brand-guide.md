# .light — Volcanic Infrastructure Asset Pack

A cohesive starter identity for `.light`, a Trycord ecosystem hosting and infrastructure platform.

## Direction
Volcanic strata, controlled heat, mineral contrast. No generic cloud imagery and no blue/purple SaaS palette. The identity uses a restrained warm spectrum balanced by mineral teal and pumice neutrals.

## Palette
- Basalt: `#111311`\n- Obsidian: `#080A09`\n- Lava: `#E34B2F`\n- Magma: `#FF7847`\n- Sulfur: `#F2C75C`\n- Copper: `#A96745`\n- Pumice: `#D8D0C3`\n- Mineral: `#4C9B8A`\n- Mineral Deep: `#234C46`\n- Ash: `#77766F`\n
## Typography
Primary: Manrope. Fallback: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif. Use compact, confident headings and highly readable body text.

## Glow
Use soft, broad ambient illumination behind important active elements. Reserve crisp highlights for active controls. Mineral teal is a secondary/system-health signal. Do not glow every surface.

## Inventory
- `logos/`: primary logo, wordmark, geometric mark, favicon
- `app-icons/`: three app-icon variants
- `backgrounds/`: 16:9 volcanic strata background
- `patterns/`: tectonic contour pattern
- `icons/`: 13 standalone SVG module icons plus sprite sheet
- `ui-elements/`: accent UI kit sample
- `guidelines/`: palette and this guide

SVGs are editable vectors. The mark is an original geometric tectonic-strata concept; review and refine before trademark or production use. Manrope is referenced by name but not bundled.
